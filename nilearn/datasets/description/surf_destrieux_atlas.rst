Destrieux atlas
===============

Access
------
See :func:`nilearn.datasets.fetch_atlas_surf_destrieux`.

Notes
-----
Anatomical :term:`parcellation` of the cortex (76 labels per hemisphere) based on sulco-gyral patterns
(:footcite:t:`Destrieux2010`) distributed with Freesurfer (:footcite:t:`Dale1999`,
:footcite:t:`Fischl1999b`) in fsaverage5 standard surface space.
